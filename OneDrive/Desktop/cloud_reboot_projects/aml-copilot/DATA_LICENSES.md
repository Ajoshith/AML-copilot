# Data licenses and provenance

This project uses three real, public data sources and generates one clearly-labelled
synthetic overlay. Every record's `source_id` prefix identifies which of these it came
from — see [src/domain/ids.ts](src/domain/ids.ts).

| Prefix | Source | License | Real? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `txn:ibm:HI-Small:*` | IBM AMLworld `HI-Small_Trans.csv` (5,078,345 labelled synthetic-agent-simulated transactions) | [CDLA-Sharing-1.0](https://cdla.dev/sharing-1-0/) | A published research benchmark with real laundering ground truth (`Is Laundering`), not a real bank's transactions |
| `sdn:ofac:*` | U.S. Treasury OFAC Specially Designated Nationals list (`sdn.csv`, `alt.csv`) | U.S. Government work, public domain | Yes — the real, current sanctions list at time of download |
| `policy:ffiec-appF:*` | FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual, Appendix F ("Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Red Flags") | U.S. Government work, public domain | Yes — real regulator-published guidance |
| `kyc:overlay:*`, `alert:overlay:*`, `note:overlay:*` | Generated by `scripts/build-overlay.ts` | N/A (synthetic) | **No** — fabricated KYC/CDD profiles, TM alerts, and analyst notes; no public dataset provides these fields without being real PII |

## CDLA-Sharing-1.0 obligations

The Community Data License Agreement – Sharing, Version 1.0 requires attribution and
requires that any published derivative of the data remain under the same license. This
repository satisfies that by:

- Not redistributing the full 475 MB `HI-Small_Trans.csv` — it is downloaded fresh by
  `scripts/fetch-data.ts` into the gitignored `data/raw/` directory.
- Committing only a small derived slice (`data/slice/transactions.parquet`, covering the
  8 mined cases' accounts) under `data/slice/`, itself a CDLA-Sharing-1.0 derivative,
  attributed here.
- Attribution: IBM Transactions for Anti-Money Laundering (AML), IBM Research, distributed
  under CDLA-Sharing-1.0. Source: [IBM/AML-Data](https://github.com/IBM/AML-Data) /
  [Hugging Face mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/eexzzm/IBM-Transactions-for-Anti-Money-Laundering-HI-Small-Trans).

## OFAC SDN list

U.S. government works are not subject to copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105).
The SDN list is downloaded fresh by `scripts/fetch-data.ts`; `data/raw/ofac-list-version.txt`
records the download date, which is stamped into every `sdn:ofac:<date>:<ent_num>` source id
as that record's list version. Source: [OFAC Sanctions List Service](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-list-service).

## FFIEC BSA/AML Manual, Appendix F

Also a U.S. government work, public domain. Scraped once by `scripts/fetch-data.ts` into
`src/policy/typologies.yaml`, versioned by `POLICY_VERSION` in `src/config.ts`. Source:
[FFIEC BSA/AML Manual — Appendix F](https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/manual/Appendices/07).

## Generated overlay data

KYC/CDD profiles, alert records, and analyst notes are synthetic — real KYC data is PII
by definition and no public equivalent exists. They are clearly labelled with an
`:overlay:` source-id segment everywhere they appear (case packets, the UI, audit logs) so
a reviewer can always tell fabricated context apart from the three real sources above. See
`scripts/build-overlay.ts` for exactly how each field is derived or generated.
